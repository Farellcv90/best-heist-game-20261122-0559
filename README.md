**The Gilded Hour** — a top-down stealth heist in three vaults. Slip past patrol guards and sweeping cameras, lift the loot, and reach the exit before the alarm fills. Coin tosses, smoke and EMPs cost favors; marble is loud, carpet is silent. Pure canvas, no libraries, every sound a WebAudio oscillator.
